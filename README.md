## Papers

- **[Performance and Efficiency Trade-Offs in Fine-Tuned and Pruned Large Language Models](https://iacis.org/iis/2026/2_iis_2026_669-684.pdf)** — Co-author, *Issues in Information Systems*. We tested how much of LLaMA we could prune while preserving its ability to generate code. At 20% structured pruning, it retained 97.6% of its baseline Pass@k performance. We presented the work at the 66th IACIS Annual Conference.
- **[Human–Robot Interactive Performance Analysis with Enhanced Metacognition](https://doi.org/10.1080/08874417.2026.2637477)** — First author, *Journal of Computer Information Systems*. I analyzed simulations of how a robot performs when it can reassess its decisions. Modeled errors fell by 59.29%, and execution time rose by 18.67%.
